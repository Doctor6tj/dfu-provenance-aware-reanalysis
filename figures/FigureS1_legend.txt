Supplementary Figure S1. Supporting participant-level single-cell analysis of GSE165816 foot skin. (A) Outcome-blinded UMAP of 39,238 retained singlets with marker-based cell-type labels. UMAP coordinates are descriptive and their axes have no biological scale. (B) Participant-level cell fractions for the eight cell types that met the prespecified differential-expression eligibility rule. Boxes and points are descriptive; no abundance P values were calculated. (C) Minimum globally adjusted FDR within each eligible cell type. Across 65,624 gene-by-cell-type tests, no association passed the single global Benjamini-Hochberg correction (minimum global FDR, 0.309). The comparison included seven healers and four nonhealers and was supporting rather than independent replication.
