Figure 1. Provenance-aware study design and evidence hierarchy. Six public human diabetic-foot transcriptomic series were mapped from 127 accession records to 94 conservative analytic units. GSE80178 supplied the core within-study bulk contrasts, whereas GSE165816 supplied supporting participant-level single-cell context. GSE68183 was retained as an exact-reuse source; GSE134431, GSE143735, and GSE199939 remained outside the quantitative core. Specimen-level ulcer status was unresolved for GSE199939. Only one independent compatible core bulk study remained, so cross-study gene and pathway robustness were not estimable. No single-cell association passed global correction; this does not establish biological absence.
