Figure 4. Evidence tiers and the boundary of cross-study robustness. (A) Of 18,865 tested genes, 18,856 had no threshold-level signal and nine appeared only in n=11 sensitivity analyses; no gene met the cross-study robustness definition. The small sensitivity subset is annotated because it occupies 0.048% of the gene universe. (B) Magnification of the nine sensitivity-only genes shows one separate-comparator signal and eight additional pooled-only signals. (C) Two accession labels collapsed to one independent compatible study, below the prespecified minimum of two. Pathway robustness was therefore not estimable. The result describes insufficient independent compatible evidence and does not establish biological absence.
